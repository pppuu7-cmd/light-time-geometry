# LTG Core Hypothesis — v0.1

## Established starting point

In special relativity,

\[
E^2-p^2c^2=m^2c^4,
\]

and for a timelike worldline,

\[
d\tau = dt\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}.
\]

For a null worldline,

\[
ds^2=0, \qquad d\tau=0.
\]

These are standard facts and are not themselves LTG claims.

## Exploratory hypothesis H1

There may exist a deeper covariant structure whose observable projections include:

- a null/radiative sector associated with propagation at the invariant speed `c`;
- a timelike/massive sector associated with nonzero proper time;
- a transition or coupling rule between these sectors that is not reducible to a change of coordinates or to the ordinary mass-shell identity.

## Stronger hypothesis H2

The appearance of nonzero proper time may be dynamically linked to stress-energy and therefore, through a covariant coupling, to spacetime geometry.

This is **not** assumed to be true. It is a target for derivation or falsification.

## Candidate bookkeeping variables

Define

\[
Q_s=pc,\qquad Q_\tau=mc^2,
\]

so

\[
E^2=Q_s^2+Q_\tau^2.
\]

No physical interpretation is granted merely from this Euclidean-looking decomposition. In particular, the underlying spacetime and four-momentum geometries remain Lorentzian.

## Minimal novelty requirement

LTG counts as more than a reinterpretation only if at least one of the following is obtained:

1. a new Lorentz-covariant degree of freedom;
2. a new invariant relation not algebraically reducible to standard SR/GR/QFT identities;
3. a new dynamical equation with the standard theory as a limit;
4. a distinct, falsifiable observable prediction.

## Immediate falsification criterion

If every proposed LTG variable can be mapped one-to-one onto ordinary rapidity, four-velocity, four-momentum, proper time, or stress-energy without introducing a new invariant or dynamical law, then that version of LTG is classified as **interpretive only** and not new physics.
