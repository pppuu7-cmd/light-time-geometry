# EXP-075 — Quantum focusing form of the generalized LTG variable

## Objective

Test whether the causal/null evolution of

\[
\mathcal N_{\rm gen}
=
\frac{S_{\rm gen}}{2\pi k_B}
\]

has a known fundamental quantum-gravitational constraint.

Use the Quantum Focusing Conjecture (QFC) / restricted quantum-focusing structure as the benchmark.

---

## 1. Generalized entropy on a null cut

For a null hypersurface with cross-sectional area element, generalized entropy is schematically

\[
S_{\rm gen}
=
\frac{A}{4G\hbar}
+
S_{\rm out}
+
\cdots
\]

in natural units for the entropy count.

Define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal N_{\rm gen}
=
\frac{S_{\rm gen}}{2\pi}.
}
\]

Let \(\lambda\) be an affine parameter along a null generator.

---

## 2. Quantum expansion

The quantum expansion is defined schematically by

\[
\Theta
\propto
\frac{G\hbar}{A}
\frac{dS_{\rm gen}}{d\lambda}.
\]

For a homogeneous cut, using

\[
S_{\rm gen}=2\pi\mathcal N_{\rm gen},
\]

one can write, up to the standard positive normalization,

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_{\rm LTG}
\propto
\frac{1}{A}
\frac{d\mathcal N_{\rm gen}}{d\lambda}.
}
\]

---

## 3. Quantum focusing

The QFC asserts, in its domain of validity, that quantum expansion is non-increasing under further null deformation:

\[
\boxed{
\frac{d\Theta}{d\lambda}
\le0.
}
\]

Therefore the LTG variable satisfies the structural inequality

\[
\boxed{
\frac{d}{d\lambda}
\left[
\frac{1}{A}
\frac{d\mathcal N_{\rm gen}}{d\lambda}
\right]
\le0
}
\]

for the homogeneous-null benchmark, modulo the precise local functional normalization used in the full QFC.

### Result 075A — quantum causal focusing of LTG information

The generalized LTG information cannot evolve arbitrarily along a quantum lightsheet.

Its appropriately area-normalized null derivative is constrained by quantum focusing.

---

## 4. Classical limit

When

\[
S_{\rm out}
\]

and quantum corrections are negligible,

\[
S_{\rm gen}
\to
\frac{A}{4G\hbar}.
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal N_{\rm gen}
\propto A,
\]

and the QFC reduces toward the classical null-focusing/Raychaudhuri structure under the usual assumptions.

Thus the same generalized variable naturally interpolates between:

- classical area focusing;
- quantum generalized-entropy focusing.

---

## 6. Does QFC provide new LTG physics?

No by itself.

QFC / quantum Bousso / generalized-second-law structures are established research programs.

Furthermore, the unrestricted QFC is subtle: recent controlled models have motivated restricted versions and identified settings in which naive formulations require refinement.

Therefore LTG cannot claim the focusing inequality as a new law.

### Result 075B

\[
\boxed{
\text{fundamental causal/quantum structure found,
but it is prior art}.
}
\]

---

## 7. What is still missing?

The two strongest known principles now constrain:

1. finite perturbations:
   \[
   D(\rho\Vert\sigma)\ge0;
   \]
2. null evolution:
   \[
   d\Theta/d\lambda\le0.
   \]

Neither uniquely determines the cosmological background trajectory

\[
\mathcal N_{\rm gen}(a)
\]

from initial data.

Therefore a genuinely new LTG law must add one nontrivial ingredient without contradicting these principles.

A valid candidate must:

- reduce to entanglement first-law balance at linear order;
- have a positive relative-entropy correction at second order;
- respect quantum focusing along null boundaries;
- predict a cosmological observable not already fixed by Einstein/modified-gravity field equations.

---

## 8. Active next gate

The remaining route is a **selection/saturation principle**, not another kinematic identity.

The most conservative possibility is to ask whether the homogeneous cosmological background is the state that minimizes the relative-entropy residual subject to its global conserved charges and causal-boundary data.

That would turn

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm grav}\ge0
\]

into a variational principle.

The next experiment should test whether such minimization simply reproduces entanglement equilibrium / Einstein gravity or generates an independent cosmological relation.
