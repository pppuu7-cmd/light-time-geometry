## 6. Why this is not yet a discovery of new physics

A new physical discovery would require at least one result not obtained by algebraically combining the known ingredients.

For example:

1. derive the horizon area spectrum rather than assume it;
2. show the same integer \(n\) is invariant under a broader class of non-de-Sitter dynamical horizons;
3. derive a transition rule \(n\to n\pm1\) with a new measurable spectrum;
4. derive a state-selection law for \(n\);
5. produce a falsifiable observable not already implied by black-hole/de-Sitter thermodynamics.

At present the formula is a powerful organizing identity, but each equality is inherited from known structures once the assumptions are made.
