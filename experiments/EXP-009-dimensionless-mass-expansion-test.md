# EXP-009 — Dimensionless mass–expansion test

## Objective

Take the strongest relation found so far,

\[
m_{\rm eff}\propto a,
\]

and determine whether it corresponds to a physical, dimensionless prediction or only to a rescaling of dimensional quantities.

## 1. Minimal 5D cosmology

Use the flat anisotropic 5D metric

\[
dS_5^2=-c^2dt^2+a(t)^2d\mathbf x^2+b(t)^2d\psi^2.
\]

Define

\[
H\equiv\frac{\dot a}{a},
\qquad
K\equiv\frac{\dot b}{b}.
\]

For vacuum 5D Einstein gravity, the `00` constraint for three equal external scale factors and one internal scale factor is

\[
3H^2+3HK=0.
\]

The nonstatic branch `H != 0` therefore obeys

\[
\boxed{K=-H}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\frac{\dot b}{b}=-\frac{\dot a}{a}}
\]

and

\[
ab=\text{const}.
\]

This reproduces the Kasner branch of EXP-005 but shows that the inverse relation follows directly from the vacuum constraint on the nonstatic branch.

## 2. Hidden momentum and effective mass

If `psi` is cyclic, its canonical momentum is conserved:

\[
\dot p_\psi=0.
\]

The 4D effective mass generated by hidden momentum is

\[
m_{\rm eff}c=\frac{|p_\psi|}{b}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{\dot m_{\rm eff}}{m_{\rm eff}}=-K.
\]

Using the 5D vacuum constraint,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\dot m_{\rm eff}}{m_{\rm eff}}=H
}
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{m_{\rm eff}\propto a}.
\]

This is the cleanest mathematical realization found so far of the original intuition linking the massive/timelike sector to expansion of the observed dimensions.

## 3. The dimensional-quantity trap

The equation `m_eff propto a` is not by itself an observable, because mass is dimensional and the scale factor normalization is conventional.

A real test requires a dimensionless ratio.

## 4. 4D Planck scale after compactification

For a compact fifth direction with physical size proportional to `b`, dimensional reduction of the 5D Einstein–Hilbert term gives schematically

\[
M_{P,4}^2\propto b\,M_5^3
\]

when the underlying 5D Planck scale `M_5` and coordinate period are fixed.

Hence in the corresponding Jordan-frame description

\[
M_{P,4}\propto b^{1/2}.
\]

For the vacuum branch `b propto a^{-1}`,

\[
M_{P,4}\propto a^{-1/2}.
\]

The dimensionless mass ratio is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\frac{m_{\rm eff}}{M_{P,4}}\propto a^{3/2}
}.
\]

This is no longer a statement about units alone.

Equivalently, a gravitational fine-structure parameter for such a mass,

\[
\alpha_G\sim\frac{G_4m_{\rm eff}^2}{\hbar c}
\sim\left(\frac{m_{\rm eff}}{M_{P,4}}\right)^2,
\]

would scale as

\[
\boxed{\alpha_G\propto a^3}.
\]

The same physical variation must reappear after a Weyl transformation to an Einstein frame, although it is redistributed between the Planck scale and matter masses. The dimensionless ratio is the meaningful quantity.

## 5. Immediate phenomenological implication

If ordinary particle masses were generated by this unstabilized KK mechanism, the vacuum branch would predict order-Hubble fractional evolution of dimensionless gravitational/matter couplings:

\[
\frac{d\ln(m/M_P)}{dt}=\frac32H,
\]

\[
\frac{d\ln\alpha_G}{dt}=3H.
\]

Precision tests of the stability of fundamental couplings and equivalence-principle tests strongly constrain cosmological scalar/modulus evolution coupled to ordinary matter. Therefore this simple unstabilized branch cannot be treated as a viable model of ordinary matter without a detailed screening/stabilization mechanism.

This is a falsification success: the model now makes a dimensionless statement that can in principle be confronted with data.

## 6. Escape routes and their cost

### A. Stabilize `b`

If

\[
b\to b_0,
\]

then

\[
\dot m\to0
\]

and the strong relation `m propto a` disappears at late times.

### B. Restrict the mechanism to a hidden sector

Ordinary masses may arise from Standard Model/Higgs physics while only a dark KK sector obeys `m_KK propto1/b`. This avoids immediate identification with measured ordinary mass variation, but the model then loses the claim that all matter is generated by the mechanism.

### C. Introduce nontrivial matter/radion coupling

A conformal coupling can modify which masses and dimensionless ratios vary. But this moves the theory into standard scalar–tensor/radion model space and must be compared against that literature before any novelty claim.

## 7. Status

### F-009A

The relation

\[
\boxed{\dot m/m=H}
\]

is exact on the nonstatic flat **5D vacuum** branch with conserved hidden momentum.

### F-009B

The corresponding dimensionless prediction is not `m propto a` but, under minimal compactification assumptions,

\[
\boxed{m/M_{P,4}\propto a^{3/2}}
\]

or equivalently `alpha_G propto a^3`.

### F-009C

This strong variation is a liability rather than evidence for the model: ordinary-matter applications require stabilization, screening, or a different dynamical branch.

### F-009D

No LTG-specific novelty has yet been established. The result is a concrete, falsifiable consequence of the simplest higher-dimensional realization and provides a much sharper target for the next stage.
