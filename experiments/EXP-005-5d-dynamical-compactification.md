# EXP-005 — 5D dynamical compactification and observable-space growth

## Question

EXP-004 showed that a 5D null trajectory can project to a 4D timelike trajectory with

\[
c\,d\tau=b(t)|dy|
\]

for an extra-dimensional metric coefficient `b(t)`, and that conserved momentum in the hidden direction can appear as 4D rest mass.

EXP-005 asks:

> Can higher-dimensional Einstein dynamics correlate expansion of the visible three-space with contraction/evolution of the hidden direction?

If yes, this would realize a geometrical version of the original LTG idea in which the same underlying geometry controls 4D proper time/mass and spatial expansion.

This experiment deliberately starts with the simplest known case: 5D vacuum Kasner dynamics. The goal is to identify the exact relation and determine whether it is new.

---

## Metric ansatz

Take

\[
dS_5^2=-c^2dt^2+a(t)^2(d x^2+d y^2+d z^2)+b(t)^2d\psi^2,
\]

where:

- `a(t)` is the visible 3D scale factor;
- `b(t)` is the scale factor of one spacelike internal dimension `psi`.

To avoid confusing the visible Cartesian coordinate `y` with the hidden coordinate used in EXP-004, the internal coordinate is called `psi` here.

For a vacuum 5D Bianchi-I/Kasner solution, take power laws

\[
a(t)\propto t^p,
\qquad
b(t)\propto t^q.
\]

With three equal visible exponents and one internal exponent, the vacuum Kasner constraints are

\[
3p+q=1,
\]

\[
3p^2+q^2=1.
\]

Solving gives two branches:

\[
(p,q)=(0,1)
\]

and

\[
\boxed{(p,q)=\left(\frac12,-\frac12\right)}.
\]

The nontrivial branch therefore has

\[
\boxed{a(t)\propto t^{1/2}},
\qquad
\boxed{b(t)\propto t^{-1/2}}.
\]

Thus the visible three-space expands while the internal direction contracts.

---

## First important relation

For the nontrivial vacuum branch,

\[
a(t)b(t)=\text{constant}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{\frac{\dot a}{a}=-\frac{\dot b}{b}.}
\]

So a direct inverse relation between external expansion and internal contraction occurs without introducing 5D matter.

This is a known higher-dimensional compactification behavior, not a new LTG result.

---

## 4D effective mass from hidden momentum

Because `psi` is a cyclic coordinate in the simple ansatz, its covariant momentum `p_psi` is conserved along a geodesic.

For a 5D massless/null mode,

\[
G^{AB}P_AP_B=0.
\]

Separating the 4D and hidden parts gives

\[
g^{\mu\nu}p_\mu p_\nu+\frac{p_\psi^2}{b(t)^2}=0.
\]

A 4D observer writes

\[
g^{\mu\nu}p_\mu p_\nu=-m_{eff}(t)^2c^2.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
m_{eff}(t)=\frac{|p_\psi|}{b(t)c}
}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\frac{\dot m_{eff}}{m_{eff}}
=-\frac{\dot b}{b}.
\]

On the nontrivial Kasner branch,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\dot m_{eff}}{m_{eff}}
=\frac{\dot a}{a}
}
\]

and consequently

\[
\boxed{m_{eff}\propto a.}
\]

This is a remarkably compact realization of a mass/geometry correlation:

\[
\boxed{
\text{visible space expands}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{internal space contracts}
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{4D KK mass scale grows}
}
\]

within this specific 5D vacuum solution.

## Proper-time relation

For a 5D null curve,

\[
0=dS_5^2=ds_4^2+b(t)^2d\psi^2.
\]

If the 4D projection is timelike,

\[
ds_4^2=-c^2d\tau^2,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{c\,d\tau=b(t)|d\psi|.}
\]

Therefore the same internal metric factor `b(t)` controls both:

1. the conversion between hidden displacement and 4D proper time;
2. the effective 4D mass scale generated by hidden momentum.

Specifically,

\[
m_{eff}\propto b^{-1},
\qquad
\frac{d\tau}{|d\psi|}\propto b.
\]

Their product is independent of `b` for fixed hidden momentum:

\[
\boxed{
m_{eff}\,\frac{d\tau}{|d\psi|}=\frac{|p_\psi|}{c^2}
}
\]

(up to the chosen coordinate normalization).

This reciprocal structure is potentially useful for LTG because it ties mass and proper-time conversion to the same geometric modulus.

---

## Relation to standard 4D cosmology

The visible scale factor

\[
a(t)\propto t^{1/2}
\]

has the same power-law form as a spatially flat radiation-dominated 4D FRW universe.

Thus a 5D vacuum geometry can project to visible expansion with a familiar 4D radiation-like scaling.

This does **not** mean the Standard Model radiation era is explained by this solution. It only demonstrates that lower-dimensional effective matter/expansion can arise from higher-dimensional geometry.

---

## Does this realize the original LTG idea?

It realizes a sharpened geometric analogue:

- the fundamental 5D motion can be null;
- hidden momentum appears as 4D rest mass;
- hidden displacement appears as 4D proper-time interval;
- 5D vacuum dynamics can make visible space expand while the hidden dimension contracts;
- in the simplest Kasner branch the induced mass scale grows in direct proportion to the visible scale factor.

However, every one of these ingredients belongs to known higher-dimensional/Kaluza-Klein physics.

Therefore this is **not evidence of new physics**, but it is the first framework found in this project in which all three original motifs — lightlike motion, proper time/mass, and expansion — appear in one coherent geometry.

---

## Phenomenological warning

If `m_eff proportional to a` applied literally to observed Standard Model rest masses throughout late cosmological history, particle masses would vary strongly as the universe expands. That is not part of standard phenomenology and would require confrontation with tight observational bounds on varying masses/couplings.

Therefore a viable version would need at least one of:

1. stabilization of `b(t)` before epochs in which masses are tightly measured;
2. the KK mass relation to apply only to a hidden sector;
3. a mechanism separating observed Higgs-generated masses from the geometric KK scale;
4. a much more subtle effective 4D frame in which dimensionless observables remain consistent with data.

## EXP-005 result

### Mathematical result

For the nontrivial 5D vacuum Kasner branch,

\[
\boxed{a\propto t^{1/2},\quad b\propto t^{-1/2},\quad ab=\text{const}.}
\]

For a 5D null mode with conserved hidden momentum,

\[
\boxed{m_{eff}\propto b^{-1}\propto a.}
\]

And the 5D null condition gives

\[
\boxed{c\,d\tau=b|d\psi|.}
\]
