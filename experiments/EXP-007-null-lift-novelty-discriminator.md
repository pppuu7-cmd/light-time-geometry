# EXP-007 — Null-lift novelty discriminator and phenomenology

## Objective

Determine whether the 5D null-lift picture developed in EXP-004–006 contains physical content beyond a tautological embedding of 4D proper time and beyond standard Kaluza–Klein/radion theory.

## 1. Tautology test

Take any 4D timelike worldline with signature `(-,+,+,+)`:

\[
ds_4^2=-c^2d\tau^2.
\]

Introduce an auxiliary coordinate

\[
\psi\equiv c\tau
\]

and the product metric

\[
dS_5^2=ds_4^2+d\psi^2.
\]

Then identically

\[
dS_5^2=-c^2d\tau^2+c^2d\tau^2=0.
\]

Therefore the statement

> every 4D timelike curve can be represented as a 5D null curve by defining `psi=c tau`

is mathematically true but **contains no new dynamics**. It is an embedding identity.

### Consequence

The relations

\[
c\,d\tau=|d\psi|
\]

and, by themselves, any conclusions obtained only after setting `psi=c tau`, cannot count as new LTG physics.

For the fifth coordinate to have independent physical meaning, at least one of the following must be specified independently of `tau`:

- topology or periodicity of `psi`;
- a dynamical metric component `b(x)`;
- an independent field equation in the higher-dimensional space;
- a conserved/quantized momentum `p_psi`;
- interactions whose observable predictions depend on `psi` or `b`.

## 2. Independent 5D mass-shell test

For

\[
dS_5^2=g_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu dx^\nu+b^2d\psi^2
\]

consider an independently defined 5D null momentum:

\[
G^{AB}P_AP_B=0.
\]

Then

\[
g^{\mu\nu}p_\mu p_\nu+\frac{p_\psi^2}{b^2}=0.
\]

A 4D observer defines effective rest mass by

\[
g^{\mu\nu}p_\mu p_\nu=-m_{\rm eff}^2c^2,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{m_{\rm eff}^2c^2=\frac{p_\psi^2}{b^2}}.
\]

This step is not a consequence of `psi=c tau`; it follows from an independent higher-dimensional mass-shell condition. However, it is the standard Kaluza–Klein/null-reduction mechanism: hidden momentum appears as 4D mass.

## 3. Field-level equivalence map

For a massless 5D scalar,

\[
\Box_5\Phi=0,
\]

with a separable mode

\[
\Phi(x,\psi)=\phi_n(x)e^{ik_n\psi},
\]

one obtains, for constant `b`,

\[
\left(\Box_4-\frac{k_n^2}{b^2}\right)\phi_n=0.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{m_n c/\hbar=|k_n|/b}.
\]

If the fifth direction is periodic, `psi ~ psi + 2 pi R_coord`, then

\[
k_n=\frac{n}{R_{\rm coord}},
\]

and the physical compactification radius is `R_phys=b R_coord`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{m_n=\frac{|n|\hbar}{cR_{\rm phys}}}.
\]

This is the ordinary KK tower. No LTG-specific observable has appeared yet.

## 4. Dimensionless-observable test

Variation of a dimensional quantity such as `m_eff` is not by itself an invariant observable. We must form dimensionless ratios.

### 4.1 Ratios inside one pure KK tower

For modes generated by the same compact radius,

\[
m_n/m_j=|n|/|j|.
\]

This ratio is independent of `b`. Therefore a common rescaling of the entire tower does not by itself provide an observable LTG signature.

The Compton-to-compactification ratio is likewise fixed:

\[
\frac{\lambda_{C,n}}{R_{\rm phys}}
=\frac{\hbar/(m_nc)}{R_{\rm phys}}
=\frac1{|n|}.
\]

Again this is not a new varying observable.

### 4.2 Comparison with an independent reference sector

A physical variation appears only if some independent scale `M_ref` does **not** scale in the same way:

\[
\frac{m_n}{M_{\rm ref}}\propto\frac1{bM_{\rm ref}}.
\]

The model must therefore specify how ordinary matter, gauge scales, the Planck scale, and the radion couple. Without that information the statement `m_eff propto 1/b` is incomplete phenomenology.

## 5. Stabilization test

The vacuum Kasner branch used in EXP-005 gave

\[
a\propto t^{1/2},\qquad b\propto t^{-1/2},\qquad ab=\text{const},
\]

hence, for conserved `p_psi`,

\[
m_{\rm eff}\propto1/b\propto a.
\]

But this relation is branch-specific, not universal.

Introduce a radion variable schematically

\[
\sigma\equiv\ln(b/b_0)
\]

with a 4D effective action of the form

\[
S_{\rm eff}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[
\frac{M_P^2}{2}R-\frac12(\partial\sigma)^2-V(\sigma)
\right]+S_{\rm matter}[g,\sigma].
\]

If

\[
V'(0)=0,\qquad V''(0)>0,
\]

then the compact radius can settle to

\[
b\to b_0.
\]

Consequently

\[
m_n\to \frac{|n|\hbar}{cR_{\rm phys,0}}=\text{const}.
\]

Therefore stabilizing the extra dimension destroys the simple vacuum relation `m_eff propto a` unless a residual radion/matter coupling remains.

## 6. Observable classification

At the present level, every surviving effect belongs to an already-known class:

- KK mass towers from hidden momentum;
- radion-dependent masses;
- varying dimensionless ratios when different sectors couple differently to the radion;
- scalar/fifth-force phenomenology from an unstabilized radion;
- cosmological evolution of compactification moduli.

No invariant observable unique to LTG has yet survived.

## Result

### F-007A — particle-level null lift

**Classification: tautological if `psi=c tau` is imposed by definition.**

### F-007B — independent 5D momentum and field modes

**Classification: physically nontrivial but known-equivalent to standard Kaluza–Klein/null-reduction physics at this level.**

### F-007C — `m_eff propto a` on the vacuum branch

**Classification: branch-dependent and not robust under radion stabilization.**

### F-007D — novelty status

No LTG-specific dimensionless observable has been found yet.

## Next step

1. `m=0` gives the null/light sector;
2. `m>0` gives the timelike sector;
3. the 5D null equation reduces exactly to the 4D massive equation;
4. any proposed extension is tested against standard KK theory before being called new.

This is EXP-008.
