# EXP-099 — Independent particle-physics scale tests for the de Sitter information hierarchy

## Objective

Ask whether known microscopic particle-physics scales can independently generate a de Sitter entropy count near

\[
10^{122}
\]

without using the Hubble constant or cosmological dark-energy density as input.

---

## 1. Neutrino/WGC benchmark

Quantum-gravity compactification arguments based on the Weak Gravity Conjecture have related a positive 4D cosmological constant to the neutrino mass scale, schematically

\[
\rho_\Lambda
\gtrsim O(m_\nu^4).
\]

If one takes

\[
\rho_X\sim m^4,
\]

then in de Sitter

\[
\boxed{
\frac{S_{\rm dS}}{k_B}
=
24\pi^2
\left(
\frac{\bar M_P}{m}
\right)^4,
}
\]

where

\[
\bar M_P=(8\pi G)^{-1/2}.
\]

Using particle-oscillation scales only:

### Solar splitting scale

\[
m_{\rm solar}\simeq8.6\,{\rm meV}
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm dS}/k_B
\sim1.5\times10^{120}.
}
\]

### Atmospheric splitting scale

\[
m_{\rm atm}\simeq50\,{\rm meV}
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{
S_{\rm dS}/k_B
\sim1.3\times10^{117}.
}
\]

These are large but do not uniquely produce the LTG target count.

To obtain

\[
S/k_B
=
3.307\times10^{122}
\]

from a pure \(m^4\) law would require

\[
\boxed{
m_{\rm eff}
\simeq2.24\,{\rm meV}.
}
\]

The absolute lightest neutrino mass is not fixed by oscillation data.

### Result 099A

Particle physics can naturally generate a hierarchy in the \(10^{117}-10^{120}\) range using known neutrino splittings, but the exact \(10^{122}\) value is not independently determined.

---

## 2. QCD-gravity benchmark

There are longstanding proposals of the schematic form

\[
\rho_X
\sim
G\Lambda_{\rm QCD}^6.
\]

Such a relation can generate a very small vacuum scale from QCD dimensional transmutation plus gravity.

However the predicted entropy count depends strongly on the effective QCD scale and the model-dependent coefficient.

Thus the relation explains how a huge hierarchy can emerge from Standard-Model quantum dynamics but does not give a unique parameter-free value.

---

## 3. Consequence

The particle sector demonstrates an important possibility:

\[
\boxed{
10^{122}
\text{ need not be a fundamental input}.
}
\]

It can arise as a large power/exponential of much smaller microscopic hierarchies.

But no established Standard-Model + quantum-gravity relation uniquely fixes the required number.

---

\boxed{\text{NOT OBTAINED}.}
