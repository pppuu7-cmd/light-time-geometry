# EXP-020 — Prior-art map and novelty boundary

## Objective

Before introducing any new LTG postulate, compare the strongest relations found in EXP-001–019 against nearby established research programs.

The purpose is adversarial: if a proposed LTG ingredient already exists elsewhere, classify it as prior art rather than treating a new interpretation as a new physical law.

## 1. Proper-time action phase

For a free relativistic particle,

\[
S=-mc^2\int d\tau,
\]

so the quantum phase is

\[
\boxed{\Phi=\frac{S}{\hbar}=-\frac{mc^2}{\hbar}\int d\tau.}
\]

Therefore the LTG phase element

\[
mc^2d\tau/\hbar
\]

is not a new object. It is the standard relativistic worldline action phase.

### Classification

**Established relativistic quantum mechanics / path-integral structure.**

## 2. de Broglie / Compton internal clock

The rest-energy frequency

\[
\omega_C=mc^2/\hbar
\]

and the corresponding phase

\[
\omega_C\tau
\]

have long motivated de Broglie internal-clock interpretations and modern cyclic/periodic particle models.

However, the existence of the action phase does not by itself prove that a massive particle contains a directly observable physical oscillator ticking at `omega_C`.

Atom-interferometer literature contains both proper-time phase formulations and explicit cautions against identifying the interferometer with a literal oscillator referenced to the Compton frequency.

### Classification

**Phase established; literal-clock interpretation model dependent.**

## 3. Mass and proper time as conjugate variables

Several extended formulations of relativistic quantum mechanics promote proper time to a dynamical variable or evolution parameter and associate a mass-like Hamiltonian/operator with translations in that variable.

This includes work explicitly discussing mass and proper time as conjugate observables and earlier proper-time derivative / indefinite-mass frameworks.

Thus the conceptual pairing

\[
\boxed{\text{mass}\leftrightarrow\text{generator of proper-time evolution}}
\]

is prior art.

### Classification

**Established extended-mechanics research direction.**

## 4. Stueckelberg–Horwitz–Piron (SHP) mechanics

SHP theory introduces an invariant evolution parameter `tau` distinct from the coordinate time `x^0` and allows events to evolve off shell in an extended phase space.

Its gauge extension contains five gauge fields/components associated with spacetime plus the invariant evolution parameter. In this framework interactions can exchange mass between particles and fields, and a Maxwell-like equilibrium/on-shell limit can be recovered.

This comes very close to several verbal LTG motifs:

- an additional time/evolution-like direction;
- variable/off-shell mass;
- a 5-component gauge structure;
- dynamical return toward an on-shell mass sector.

It differs from the simplest LTG/KK route because the fifth structure is an invariant evolution parameter rather than an ordinary compact spacelike Kaluza–Klein circle.

### Classification

**Major prior-art neighbor. Any LTG proposal with independent proper-time evolution or variable mass must be compared explicitly with SHP.**

## 5. Kaluza–Klein hidden momentum → mass and charge

In standard KK theory, hidden momentum contributes to the effective 4D mass shell, and restoring the off-diagonal metric identifies hidden momentum with a 4D gauge charge.

The Hamilton–Jacobi treatment gives invariant 4D definitions of the effective mass and charge and has been used to study their possible variation in noncompact/generalized 5D settings.

Thus

\[
mc\sim |p_\psi|/b
\]

and

\[
q\sim p_\psi
\]

are established higher-dimensional mechanisms.

### Classification

**Standard KK / generalized 5D prior art.**

## 6. 5D null → 4D timelike projection

A massless/null higher-dimensional trajectory or field mode projecting to a massive/timelike lower-dimensional excitation is a standard null-lift/KK phenomenon.

Likewise,

\[
\Box_5\Phi=0
\]

reducing to a massive 4D Klein–Gordon equation for nonzero hidden momentum is standard dimensional reduction.

### Classification

**Established.**

## 7. Radiation/matter trace and geometry

The chain

\[
\text{radiation }T=0,
\qquad
\text{nonrelativistic matter }T\simeq-\rho,
\]

followed by trace/radion/scalar response is ordinary GR/scalar–tensor physics.

The LTG hidden-pressure identity

\[
T_{(4)}=-P_\psi
\]

under a traceless 5D parent stress is a compact dimensional-reduction statement, not a new interaction law.

### Classification

**Established higher-dimensional stress decomposition.**

## 8. Proper-time phase and atom interferometry

Modern atom-interferometry proposals test relativistic proper-time and mass-energy effects using quantum superpositions of paths/internal states.

This provides a realistic experimental arena for proper-time-sensitive phases. But it also reinforces an important caution: observing an interferometric phase consistent with relativistic action does not automatically establish a literal physical oscillation at the Compton frequency.

### Classification

**Active experimental/theoretical field; useful as a test language, not evidence for LTG-specific physics.**

## 10. Novelty boundary after EXP-020

The current LTG construction is best classified as

\[
\boxed{\text{a coherent synthesis / geometric interpretation of known structures}}
\]

rather than a distinct physical theory.

This is not a failure. It is a useful result because the project has reduced a broad intuition to a small number of mathematically exact relations and identified the surrounding theory space.

## 11. What would now count as genuinely new

A future LTG extension must provide at least one of:

1. a new invariant constraint not equivalent to a coordinate choice, mass shell, KK quantization, SHP evolution, or scalar–tensor equation;
2. a dimensionless observable not predicted by the standard neighboring theories;
3. a new symmetry or conservation law with an empirically distinct consequence;
4. a derivation of a known parameter/quantum number that neighboring theories merely assume;
5. a controlled limit reproducing SR/GR/QFT plus a falsifiable deviation.

## 12. Constraint-first rule

From this point forward, LTG should not add arbitrary operators simply to become different.

Any candidate postulate must be stated first as a sharp equation and tested in this order:

1. covariance/symmetry;
2. dimensional consistency;
3. flat-space limit;
4. conservation laws;
5. field-redefinition equivalence;
6. comparison with KK/SHP/scalar–tensor/QFT;
7. dimensionless observable;
8. existing experimental bounds.

If it fails any early test, reject it before building a larger model around it.
