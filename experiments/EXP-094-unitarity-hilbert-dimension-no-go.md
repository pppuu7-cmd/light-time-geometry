# EXP-094 — Unitarity no-go for literal creation of Hilbert-space dimension

## Objective

Clarify the physical meaning of "space/information growth" in LTG.

If sector \(n\) has

\[
\dim\mathcal H_n=q^n
\]

and sector \(n+1\) has

\[
\dim\mathcal H_{n+1}=q^{n+1},
\]

can a closed fundamental quantum system literally evolve unitarily from one entire Hilbert space into the larger one?

---

## 1. Unitary maps preserve dimension

A unitary map

\[
U:\mathcal H\to\mathcal H
\]

is invertible and preserves the inner product.

For finite-dimensional spaces, a unitary is an isomorphism.

Therefore a unitary map

\[
U:\mathcal H_n\to\mathcal H_{n+1}
\]

can exist only if

\[
\dim\mathcal H_n
=
\dim\mathcal H_{n+1}.
\]

But q-ary sector growth has

\[
q^n\ne q^{n+1}.
\]

Hence:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a closed unitary theory cannot literally create new fundamental Hilbert-space dimension at each }n\to n+1.
}
\]

### Result 094A

Literal fundamental-state creation is incompatible with fixed-Hilbert-space unitary quantum mechanics.

---

## 2. Allowed interpretations

Capacity growth can still occur in at least three standard ways.

### A. Nested accessible subspaces

There is a fixed larger Hilbert space

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm tot},
\]

with

\[
\mathcal H_n
\subset
\mathcal H_{n+1}
\subset
\mathcal H_{\rm tot}.
\]

Cosmic evolution changes which degrees of freedom are causally accessible.

### B. Tensor-factor accessibility

One can write schematically

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm tot}
=
\mathcal H_{\rm accessible}(t)
\otimes
\mathcal H_{\rm inaccessible}(t)
\]

in an approximate code/subsystem sense.

The accessible factor can grow while the total dimension remains fixed.

### C. Open-system/isometric description

An effective subsystem can gain dimension through coupling to ancillas/environment while the enlarged total evolution remains unitary.

---

## 3. Consequence for "energy becomes space"

The refined physical statement cannot be:

\[
\boxed{
\text{energy creates brand-new fundamental quantum states}.
}
\]

A unitary-compatible version is instead:

\[
\boxed{
\text{dynamics changes causal accessibility / entanglement / code capacity,
which is represented macroscopically as growing geometry}.
}
\]

This is much closer to holographic/emergent-spacetime frameworks.

---

## 4. Early-universe implication

A Planck-scale early causal patch can have very small **accessible** horizon entropy while the total global quantum state lives in a much larger Hilbert space.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{small early geometric area does not imply a small total fundamental state space}.
}
\]

This is important for the original "hot point without space" intuition.

A small early causal geometry can reveal only a tiny part of a much larger global quantum capacity.

---

## 5. Finite future endpoint

If

\[
D_{\rm tot}<\infty,
\]

then accessibility growth can saturate at

\[
\dim\mathcal H_{\rm accessible}
\to
D_{\rm tot}.
\]

This provides the unitary interpretation of EXP-093:

\[
\boxed{
\text{de Sitter equilibrium}
=
\text{maximum accessible causal capacity},
}
\]

not creation of the final Hilbert space from nothing.

---

## 6. Relation to holographic code-subspace physics

Holographic quantum error correction already distinguishes:

- a fixed microscopic boundary Hilbert space;
- effective bulk code subspaces;
- state-dependent geometric entanglement wedges.

Thus the allowed LTG interpretation has substantial prior art.

The LTG-specific question is whether the accessible-capacity growth obeys the causal action/detailed-balance laws derived in this project.
