# EXP-051 — Causal-diamond entanglement equilibrium and the cosmological-constant gap

## Objective

Test the third candidate left by EXP-048:

\[
\text{causal-diamond stationarity}
\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\text{a unique de Sitter scale}.
\]

The benchmark is entanglement equilibrium / gravitational thermodynamics of small causal diamonds.

---

## 1. Known causal-diamond equilibrium result

For a small geodesic ball / causal diamond, the generalized entropy is schematically

\[
S_{\rm gen}
=
\frac{A}{4G}
+
S_{\rm matter}.
\]

Entanglement-equilibrium constructions impose stationarity of the vacuum generalized entropy at fixed volume:

\[
\boxed{
\delta S_{\rm gen}\big|_V=0.
}
\]

For suitable matter assumptions, imposing this for all sufficiently small diamonds is equivalent to the semiclassical Einstein equation.

Thus causal-diamond equilibrium can recover gravitational field dynamics from entropy stationarity.

### Result 051A

Causal-diamond stationarity is strong enough to recover Einstein dynamics.

This is established prior art, not an LTG-specific result.

---

## 2. Does it fix the cosmological constant?

No.

The maximally symmetric reference vacuum contains a local curvature/cosmological-constant parameter.

When the equilibrium condition is imposed across all local diamonds, consistency plus the Bianchi identity yields the Einstein equation up to one overall spacetime cosmological constant.

Schematically,

\[
G_{\mu\nu}
+
\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}
=
8\pi G T_{\mu\nu},
\]

but the local entanglement-equilibrium condition does not determine the numerical value of the global \(\Lambda\).

This matches EXP-050's null-blindness result: local stress/entanglement variations determine the nontrivial tensor structure, while the metric-proportional vacuum offset remains globally undetermined.

### Result 051B

\[
\boxed{
\text{local causal-diamond equilibrium}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{unique }\Lambda.
}
\]

It recovers the form of Einstein dynamics but leaves the cosmological-constant scale as global data.

---

## 3. Why this matters for LTG

The desired LTG chain is

\[
\text{null/causal geometry}
\to
HL=1
\to
w=-1.
\]

EXP-051 shows that even a much stronger local principle,

\[
\delta S_{\rm gen}|_V=0
\]

for all small diamonds, does not fix the absolute vacuum curvature scale.

Therefore an LTG novelty claim cannot come merely from rediscovering entanglement equilibrium.

The new ingredient must be genuinely global:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a relation among diamonds / horizons / boundary data
that fixes the remaining integration constant}.
}
\]

---

## 4. Comparison with the three candidate gates

### Horizon entropy stationarity — EXP-049

Can select

\[
w\to-1
\]

if a finite nonzero horizon scale exists, but does not fix \(H_*\).

### Null Raychaudhuri regularity — EXP-050

Cannot see \(\Lambda\) locally at all.

### Causal-diamond entanglement equilibrium — EXP-051

Can recover Einstein dynamics but leaves one global cosmological-constant parameter.

All three independent routes therefore point to the same conclusion.

---

## 5. Convergent no-go / opportunity

The local and quasi-local gates converge on:

\[
\boxed{
\text{local physics fixes dynamics,
global causal data must fix the vacuum scale.}
}
\]

This is stronger than the earlier qualitative claim because it survives three distinct formalisms:

1. horizon entropy;
2. null focusing;
3. causal-diamond entanglement equilibrium.

The remaining research target is now sharply defined:

\[
\boxed{
\text{find a global consistency relation that fixes the
metric-proportional integration constant.}
}
\]

Possible global data include:

- total generalized entropy on a complete causal patch;
- relation between past and future null boundaries;
- global phase/winding data;
- topological sector;
- finite causal-diamond information capacity;
- a boundary condition connecting the hot early universe to the future horizon.

---

## 6. Terminal classification

### Causal-diamond stationarity recovers Einstein dynamics

**PASS / PRIOR ART.**

### Causal-diamond stationarity uniquely fixes \(\Lambda\)

**FAIL.**

### Combined LTG conclusion

\[
\boxed{
\Lambda\text{ / }H_*
\text{ is precisely the part not fixed by the tested local/null principles.}
}
\]

This makes the next gate genuinely global rather than another local operator search.
