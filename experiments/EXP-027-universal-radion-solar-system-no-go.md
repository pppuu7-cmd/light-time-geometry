# EXP-027 — Universal pure-KK mass and the long-range radion no-go

## Objective

EXP-026 showed that composition-dependent hidden-mass fractions are constrained by differential free-fall tests. But a special loophole remains:

\[
f_A=f_B=1
\]

for every matter species.

Then all masses scale identically with the compactification radius and differential scalar charges vanish. This experiment asks whether such a **universal pure-KK** scenario can evade equivalence-principle tests while keeping an unstabilized/light radion.

## 1. Pure hidden-momentum mass

For a pure KK state in the inherited frame,

\[
m_J\propto b^{-1}.
\]

After the Jordan-to-Einstein transformation used in EXP-018,

\[
m_E\propto b^{-3/2}.
\]

With canonical radion

\[
\varphi
=\sqrt{\frac32}M_{\rm Pl}\ln b,
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
m_E(\varphi)
\propto
\exp\left[
-\sqrt{\frac32}
\frac{\varphi}{M_{\rm Pl}}
\right].
}
\]

Thus the scalar coupling is not small; it is of gravitational order.

## 2. Universal coupling removes composition dependence but not the scalar force

If every species has exactly the same pure-KK dependence, then

\[
\Delta\alpha_{AB}=0.
\]

Therefore ideal composition-difference tests such as MICROSCOPE are not directly sensitive to the universal part through an Eotvos signal.

But the radion still mediates an additional scalar interaction between all massive bodies.

A universal scalar coupling changes post-Newtonian gravity, gravitational time delay, orbital dynamics, and the effective Newton constant even when the weak equivalence principle between compositions is preserved.

## 3. Standard scalar-tensor mapping

Simple dimensional reduction of higher-dimensional Einstein gravity with an unstabilized size modulus produces a scalar-tensor theory in four dimensions.

In a common Jordan-frame formulation, the simplest Kaluza–Klein reduction is equivalent to a Brans–Dicke-type theory with a very small/order-unity effective Brans–Dicke parameter; the classic empty `(D+1)` to `D` correspondence includes the `omega=0` case.

The exact parameterization depends on field definitions and matter couplings, but the physical statement is invariant:

\[
\boxed{
\text{an unstabilized radius modulus has an unsuppressed scalar coupling.}
}
\]

## 4. Solar-system test

For a massless scalar-tensor theory in standard Einstein/Jordan conventions, the PPN parameter `gamma` deviates from its GR value according to the scalar coupling. In the common notation reviewed by the Particle Data Group,

\[
\bar\gamma
\equiv\gamma-1
=-2\frac{\alpha_0^2}{1+\alpha_0^2}.
\]

Therefore a gravitational-strength coupling `alpha_0 = O(1)` produces an order-unity deviation from GR.

Cassini measured

\[
\gamma-1
=(2.1\pm2.3)\times10^{-5},
\]

consistent with zero.

Hence an unscreened, effectively massless radion with order-one universal coupling is excluded by many orders of magnitude.

### F-027A

**Universal coupling avoids composition-dependent WEP violation, but it does not avoid post-Newtonian scalar-gravity constraints.**

## 5. Consequence for the minimal pure-KK mass picture

If ordinary visible matter were dominantly pure hidden-momentum mass and the radius modulus remained long ranged, its universal radion coupling would be too large.

Therefore a realistic model requires at least one of:

1. radion stabilization with a sufficiently large mass;
2. strong suppression/decoupling of the scalar coupling;
3. a screening mechanism;
4. a compactification in which the light 4D scalar is absent/projected out;
5. visible-particle masses dominated by another sector so that radion sensitivity is small.

## 6. Relation to EXP-026

The phenomenology now separates into two complementary tests.

### Nonuniversal hidden fractions

If

\[
f_A\neq f_B,
\]

then

\[
\Delta\alpha_{AB}\neq0
\]

and composition-dependent free-fall / clock tests are powerful.

### Universal hidden fraction

If

\[
f_A=f_B,
\]

then differential tests may cancel, but a light scalar still alters universal gravity and is constrained by PPN/time-delay/orbital tests.

Thus simply making the hidden fraction universal does not rescue an unstabilized radion.

## 7. MICROSCOPE versus Cassini roles

These experiments probe different pieces:

- **MICROSCOPE:** exceptionally strong constraint on nonuniversality/composition dependence;
- **Cassini and other solar-system PPN tests:** strong constraint on a universal long-range scalar admixture to gravity.

This is exactly the split expected in generic dilaton/moduli/scalar-tensor phenomenology.

## 8. Falsification result

### F-027B

**The simplest scenario in which visible matter is pure KK mass and the compactification radius is a light unstabilized field is phenomenologically untenable.**

### F-027C

**A viable hidden-momentum mass model must separate the high-energy geometric origin from the observed low-energy scalar sector through stabilization/decoupling.**

### F-027D

**Once this stabilization/decoupling is performed, the ordinary low-energy mass/proper-time phase becomes even less capable of revealing its hidden-dimensional origin.**

This creates a central tension:

\[
\boxed{
\text{make the extra geometry observable}
\Rightarrow
\text{strong existing constraints},
}
\]

while

\[
\boxed{
\text{decouple it enough to satisfy constraints}
\Rightarrow
\text{low-energy LTG becomes operationally indistinguishable from ordinary mass physics}.
}
\]

## 9. Current research frontier

This tension is now more important than inventing additional equations.

A distinct LTG theory needs a protected residual observable that survives radion stabilization while not reducing to the standard 4D mass phase.

Candidate classes are extremely limited:

- topological/discrete remnant;
- heavy KK resonance spectrum;
- exact selection rule;
- non-decoupling threshold effect;
- cosmological relic from an early epoch when the hidden geometry was dynamical.

The next experiment should therefore search for **decoupling-resistant observables** rather than a light fifth force.
