# EXP-036 — Scale/conformal origin of the trace channel

## Objective

Test whether the deeper distinction uncovered by LTG is better understood as

\[
\text{scale-free sector}
\quad\leftrightarrow\quad
\text{scale-bearing sector}
\]

rather than simply

\[
\text{light}
\quad\leftrightarrow\quad
\text{mass/time}.
\]

The decisive test is the quantum trace anomaly: if a massless theory can generate a nonzero stress-energy trace, then the EXP-033 proper-time identity cannot be the universal source of the Ricci-scalar trace channel.

## 1. Classical particle result

For an isotropic classical ensemble of on-shell particles,

\[
T_{\rm kin}
=-\varepsilon+3P.
\]

EXP-033 showed

\[
\varepsilon-3P
=\varepsilon\chi_\tau,
\]

with

\[
\chi_\tau
=\left\langle
\left(\frac{d\tau}{dt}\right)^2
\right\rangle_E.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
T_{\rm kin}=-\varepsilon\chi_\tau.
}
\]

For ideal classical massless radiation,

\[
\chi_\tau=0,
\qquad
T_{\rm kin}=0.
\]

## 2. Why mass naturally creates an intrinsic scale

A nonzero rest mass defines the Compton length and time

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_C=\frac{\hbar}{mc},
\qquad
\tau_C=\frac{\hbar}{mc^2}.
}
\]

The proper-time phase is

\[
\Phi_m
=\frac{mc^2\tau}{\hbar}
=\frac{\tau}{\tau_C}.
\]

Thus mass does more than distinguish a timelike mass shell: it supplies an intrinsic scale against which proper time can accumulate a dimensionless phase.

This supports a refined LTG interpretation:

> the massive/timelike sector is scale-bearing, whereas ideal null propagation has no rest-frame Compton scale.

## 3. Classical conformal clue

For a classically conformal system with an appropriately improved stress tensor, the trace vanishes:

\[
T^\mu{}_{\mu}=0.
\]

An explicit mass term generally breaks scale/conformal invariance and contributes to the trace.

This is structurally consistent with the LTG chain

\[
m>0
\to
\text{intrinsic scale}
\to
T\neq0
\to
R-4\Lambda\neq0.
\]

However, this is not the full quantum story.

## 4. Quantum trace anomaly

Renormalization introduces a scale even when the classical Lagrangian contains no explicit mass scale.

For a gauge theory, the renormalized trace contains schematically

\[
\boxed{
T^\mu{}_{\mu}
\supset
\frac{\beta(g)}{2g}
F^a_{\rho\sigma}F_a^{\rho\sigma}
}
\]

plus explicit mass terms when present.

Thus a theory whose fundamental fields are massless can satisfy

\[
\boxed{T^\mu{}_{\mu}\neq0}
\]

because the running coupling generates a quantum scale.

In curved spacetime, conformal fields can also acquire the gravitational trace anomaly containing curvature invariants such as Weyl-squared and the Euler density (plus scheme-dependent total-derivative terms).

## 5. Consequence for EXP-033

The classical kinetic identity

\[
T_{\rm kin}=-\varepsilon\chi_\tau
\]

remains correct for its stated quasiparticle/on-shell assumptions.

But the full quantum expectation value can be decomposed schematically as

\[
\boxed{
\langle T\rangle
=-\varepsilon\chi_\tau
+T_{\rm anomaly}
+T_{\rm other}
}
\]

where `T_other` can include condensates, interaction contributions, vacuum terms, and non-kinetic pieces depending on the theory and renormalization prescription.

Einstein's trace equation therefore becomes

\[
R-4\Lambda
=-\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}\langle T\rangle,
\]

or

\[
\boxed{
R-4\Lambda
=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}
\left[
\varepsilon\chi_\tau
-T_{\rm anomaly}
-T_{\rm other}
\right].
}
\]

The sign of each non-kinetic contribution is theory/state dependent; the structural point is that the proper-time fraction no longer exhausts the trace.

## 6. Immediate falsification of an overly strong LTG statement

The statement

> nonzero Ricci-scalar trace curvature requires massive particles / nonzero proper time

is false in full quantum field theory.

A massless quantum field theory can have a nonzero trace through anomaly-generated scales.

Therefore the relation

\[
R-4\Lambda
\propto
\varepsilon\chi_\tau
\]

is a theorem for the classical on-shell kinetic sector, not a universal identity of all matter.

## 7. Stronger reformulation

A better candidate organizing principle is

\[
\boxed{
\text{trace channel}
\leftrightarrow
\text{breaking/generation of scale}
}
\]

rather than

\[
\text{trace channel}
\leftrightarrow
\text{rest mass only}.
\]

Rest mass is one source of scale. Quantum dimensional transmutation is another.

This gives a broader structural chain:

\[
\boxed{
\text{scale-free / conformal-like regime}
\to T\approx0
}
\]

versus

\[
\boxed{
\text{explicit or quantum-generated scale}
\to T\neq0
\to R-4\Lambda\neq0.
}
\]

## 8. Role of light and time after this refinement

### Light / ideal massless propagation

- null worldline;
- `d tau = 0`;
- no rest-frame Compton scale;
- classical ideal radiation has `T=0`.

### Massive timelike propagation

- `d tau > 0`;
- rest mass defines `tau_C = hbar/(m c^2)`;
- proper-time phase `tau/tau_C` accumulates;
- kinetic matter contributes to the trace.

### Quantum massless interacting sector

- elementary quanta can remain massless;
- nevertheless renormalization may generate a physical scale;
- the stress trace can be nonzero without a particle proper-time contribution.

Thus `light` and `time` are not a complete binary opposition. The more general distinction is between null/scale-free kinematics and mechanisms that generate invariant scales.

## 9. Result

### EXP-036A

The LTG proper-time/curvature theorem survives as an exact classical on-shell kinetic identity.

### EXP-036B

It is not universal in QFT because trace anomalies provide a massless route to nonzero `T` and hence to the Ricci-scalar trace channel.

### EXP-036C

The strongest surviving conceptual refinement is:

> rest mass and proper time are one realization of a broader scale-bearing sector; the gravitational trace channel responds to scale breaking/generation rather than uniquely to mass.

This is a more accurate organizing principle, but it is closely related to established conformal/trace-anomaly physics and is not yet a new LTG law.

## 10. New opportunity

Quantum dimensional transmutation offers a more interesting emergence test than ordinary rest-mass insertion.

A classically massless theory can generate a scale `Lambda_dyn`; bound states can then acquire masses of order that scale, and timelike proper-time phases emerge at the composite level.

This suggests EXP-037:

> follow a classically scale-free/massless sector through dimensional transmutation and ask whether the emergence of a dynamical scale, composite mass, proper time, and gravitational trace response can be expressed as one controlled chain.

If that chain contains no new invariant beyond standard RG/QFT + GR, LTG remains interpretive. If an independently motivated LTG relation fixes a ratio or observable that QFT leaves free, that would be genuine new content.
