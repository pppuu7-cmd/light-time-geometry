# EXP-028 — Audit of decoupling-resistant observables

## Objective

EXP-027 exposed a central tension:

- a light, strongly coupled radion makes the geometric mass origin observable but is heavily constrained;
- stabilizing/decoupling the radion makes ordinary low-energy propagation look like standard 4D massive physics.

This experiment asks whether any **protected residual observable** can survive stabilization strongly enough to distinguish the hidden-momentum origin of mass.

## 1. Candidate A — compact momentum number / KK parity

If the compact space has an exact translation symmetry,

\[
p_\psi=\frac{n\hbar}{R}
\]

is conserved.

Orbifolding or boundary structure may reduce continuous KK-number conservation to a discrete remnant such as

\[
\boxed{(-1)^n.}
\]

Such a parity can force the lightest odd mode to be stable and require odd KK states to be pair produced.

### Does radion stabilization erase it?

No. A discrete symmetry can survive even when the radius is fixed and its scalar fluctuation is heavy.

### Is it unique to LTG?

No. KK parity and related remnant symmetries are standard universal-extra-dimension phenomenology.

### Classification

**Decoupling resistant, experimentally meaningful, but standard.**

## 2. Candidate B — heavy KK tower

A stabilized compact space still predicts excited modes,

\[
m_n^2=m_0^2+\frac{n^2\hbar^2}{c^2R_{\rm phys}^2}
+\delta m_n^2.
\]

The radion can be heavy while the KK resonances remain at a finite scale.

### Observable

At sufficient center-of-mass energy one may observe

- repeated resonances/thresholds;
- correlated masses;
- characteristic decay cascades;
- pair-production rules.

### Is it unique to LTG?

No. This is the defining standard signal of compact extra dimensions.

### Classification

**Decoupling resistant above the KK threshold, but standard.**

## 3. Candidate C — topological phase / winding

EXP-013/018 found

\[
\frac1\hbar\oint mc^2d\tau
=2\pi\,\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf w
\]

for a compact internal cycle under the null-lift assumptions.

Because this is a canonical action/holonomy statement, it can survive continuous changes of the compactification radius and ordinary frame transformations.

### Observable difficulty

The phase is only source-discriminating if an experiment can access path sectors with different internal winding/momentum topology.

If all accessible states are confined to one effective 4D sector, the same phase is just the ordinary worldline phase of a particle of mass `m`.

### Is it unique to LTG?

No. Momentum/winding quantization and Wilson/holonomy phases are standard compactification topology.

### Classification

**Mathematically protected; not operationally unique in the decoupled low-energy sector.**

## 4. Candidate D — low-energy virtual threshold corrections

Even if real KK states are too heavy to produce, virtual heavy modes can generate higher-dimensional effective operators,

\[
\mathcal L_{\rm EFT}
=\mathcal L_{\rm SM/GR}
+\sum_i\frac{c_i}{M_{KK}^{d_i-4}}\mathcal O_i.
\]

These corrections can affect precision scattering, electroweak observables, flavor physics, gravity, or cosmology.

### Decoupling behavior

For an ordinary local heavy sector, effects vanish as powers of

\[
E/M_{KK}
\]

in the low-energy limit, except for renormalizations and genuinely non-decoupling/topological effects.

### Is it unique to LTG?

No. This is standard effective-field-theory behavior for heavy extra-dimensional states.

### Classification

**Potentially measurable, but not conceptually unique.**

## 5. Candidate E — cosmological relics

Even if the radion is stabilized today, the compactification geometry may have evolved in the early universe.

Possible relics include

- stable KK particles;
- altered freeze-out/freeze-in histories;
- dark radiation;
- phase-transition gravitational waves;
- moduli/radion relic abundance;
- changes in the expansion history during compactification.

### Is it unique to LTG?

No. These are standard extra-dimensional/moduli cosmology channels.

But they may be the best empirical arena if LTG is treated as a particular constrained cosmological scenario rather than as a new kinematic principle.

### Classification

**Decoupling resistant as historical memory, but highly model dependent and standard in broad class.**

## 6. Candidate F — exact mass-source relation after stabilization

Suppose LTG supplied a protected equation connecting observable quantities such as

\[
\text{KK spacing},
\quad
\text{radion coupling},
\quad
\text{phase integer},
\quad
\text{cosmological transition scale}.
\]

Such a relation could remain predictive even when individual new fields are heavy.

However the current project has **not derived such an additional relation**. Standard KK theory leaves these quantities dependent on compactification radius, stabilization potential, boundary conditions, and matter content.

### Classification

**This is the actual missing ingredient.**

## 7. Decoupling hierarchy

The current observables organize naturally by energy scale.

### Deep infrared

If radion and KK modes are decoupled:

\[
\boxed{
\text{ordinary 4D mass shell + proper-time phase}
}
\]

with no unique LTG signature.

### Intermediate precision regime

Virtual heavy modes / tiny scalar mixing may create suppressed EFT corrections.

### KK threshold

Tower structure, pair production, and discrete parity become directly visible.

### Early-universe regime

Compactification/radion evolution may leave cosmological relics.

This hierarchy is standard decoupling logic.

## 8. Strong no-go for a low-energy phase-only experiment

Consider two UV theories:

1. ordinary 4D QFT giving a particle physical mass `m`;
2. a stabilized higher-dimensional theory whose low-energy mode has the same mass `m` and no accessible companion fields.

Both produce at leading low energy

\[
E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4
\]

and

\[
\Phi=\frac1\hbar\int mc^2d\tau.
\]

Therefore no experiment sensitive **only** to this dispersion relation and phase can distinguish the UV origin.

### F-028A

**The ordinary proper-time phase is not a decoupling-resistant discriminator of the origin of mass.**

## 9. What survives best?

The ranking is:

1. **discrete/topological selection rules** — robust, but standard;
2. **heavy tower correlations** — direct, but require access to high energies;
3. **cosmological relics** — can survive to late times, but model dependent;
4. **virtual EFT corrections** — generic and suppressed;
5. **ordinary mass/proper-time phase** — maximally robust but source blind.

## 10. Result

### F-028B

**No currently derived LTG observable is simultaneously**

- low-energy;
- decoupling resistant;
- source sensitive;
- and distinct from standard KK/moduli physics.

### F-028C

**The current framework has reached theoretical closure as an interpretation unless a new protected relation among companion observables is postulated or derived.**

### F-028D

**The most scientifically efficient next move is not more unconstrained formalism. It is to choose one sharply specified phenomenological scenario and calculate a falsifiable correlated signature.**

## 11. Recommended branch for continued research

The cleanest branch is a hidden-sector model because EXP-023 already disfavored the minimal application to known charged leptons.

A minimal benchmark could contain

- one compact hidden dimension;
- a stabilized radion;
- a massless 5D parent field;
- a `Z2`/KK parity;
- zero-mode radiation capable of producing `n=+1,-1` pairs;
- stable or metastable first KK modes.

Then calculate, from a single parameter set,

1. the pair-production threshold;
2. the mass spectrum;
3. the relic equation of state;
4. the hidden-pressure fraction;
5. the cosmological abundance/evolution;
6. any visible portal required for detection.

If no observable differs from an ordinary extra-dimensional dark-sector model, LTG should remain a conceptual interpretation rather than a separate theory name.

## Literature anchors

- Universal extra dimensions: KK-number conservation/remnant KK parity, pair production, and stable lightest KK states are established phenomenology.
- Effective-field-theory decoupling of heavy compactification states is standard.
