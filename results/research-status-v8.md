## EXP-058 — path-integral state selection

On the conditional area spectrum,

\[
I_{E,n}=-2\pi n\hbar.
\]

The naive Euclidean saddle weight gives

\[
W_n\propto e^{2\pi n},
\]

which is monotonic and prefers \(n\to\infty\).

An opposite exponential weighting

\[

would instead prefer the minimum level.

Neither produces a finite large interior optimum.

A finite selected level requires extra \(n\)-dependent structure:

\[
W(n)=\mu(n)e^{\pm2\pi n}
\]

with a competing measure or boundary term.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{quantization is not state selection}.
}
\]

Euclidean gravity also has contour/conformal-factor subtleties, so the simple weighting argument should not be elevated into a complete quantum-cosmology theorem.
