# LTG Research Status v4 — photon/dark-energy branch tested through EXP-044

## New results since v3

\[

\[

### Minimal-theory result

In four-dimensional spatially flat FLRW, free Maxwell theory is conformally invariant and

\[
\nabla_\mu T_{\rm EM}^{\mu\nu}=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{Q^\nu_{\rm geom}=0}
\]

in minimal Einstein-Maxwell theory.

Ordinary expansion/redshift does not itself generate a photon-to-vacuum transfer current.

### Finite geometric IR threshold

Taking the natural expansion scale

\[
E_*=\hbar H
\]

gives today

\[
\hbar H_0\sim1.4\times10^{-33}\ {\rm eV}.
\]

A typical CMB photon is about

\[
4.4\times10^{29}
\]

times more energetic. In an asymptotic-de-Sitter estimate it would need about 68 additional e-folds, of order \(10^{12}\) years, to reach that scale.

Thus \(E\sim\hbar H\) does not naturally explain the present dark-energy epoch.

### Interacting benchmark

A phenomenological exchange

\[
Q=\Gamma H\rho_\gamma
\]

with a receiving sector \(w_X=-1\) can produce a constant asymptotic \(\rho_X\).

But the generated density depends strongly on the start boundary \(a_i\), and the model does not derive \(\Gamma\), \(a_i\), or \(w_X\).

Hence this is mathematically viable but not predictive.
