## EXP-052 — geometry-energy reciprocity

For a spherical gravitational horizon of radius \(L\), the Misner-Sharp / horizon energy scale is

\[
\boxed{
E_{\rm geom}(L)=\frac{c^4L}{2G}.
}
\]

This is the same threshold obtained from

\[
r_s=\frac{2GE}{c^4}=L.
\]

Therefore this energy is not the minimum needed to cross the region.

It is the compactness scale at which the localized energy itself creates a horizon of that size.

The literal traversal-cost law

\[
E_{\rm crossing}=E_{\rm geom}
\]

is false: arbitrarily low-energy photons can propagate through arbitrarily large flat regions.

### Exact surviving bridge

Using the light-crossing time

\[
\tau_L=\frac{L}{c}
\]

and horizon entropy

\[
S_H
=
\frac{k_B\pi c^3L^2}{G\hbar},
\]

one obtains

\[
\boxed{
E_{\rm geom}\tau_L
=
\frac{\hbar}{2\pi}\frac{S_H}{k_B}.
}
\]

At the Planck length,

\[
L=\ell_P,
\]

this becomes

\[
\boxed{
E_{\rm geom}=\frac12E_P,
\qquad
\tau_L=t_P,
\qquad
E_{\rm geom}t_P=\frac{\hbar}{2}.
}
\]

This is the strongest exact LTG-style relation obtained from the new hypothesis.

It simultaneously links:

\[
\boxed{
\text{energy}
+
\text{light-crossing time}
+
\text{geometric area}
+
\text{entropy}.
}
\]

It does not prove literal conversion of energy into spacetime, but it provides a clean reciprocity relation.

\[

\[

}
\]

\[
\boxed{

=
\frac{\pi c^5}{G\hbar N_*}.
}
\]

Therefore a global causal principle that uniquely fixes \(N_*\) would automatically fix the asymptotic vacuum curvature scale.

For a representative late-time de Sitter scale,

\[

\sim3\times10^{122}.
\]

By contrast, a Planck-radius horizon has

\[

\]

So the naive rule

\[

fails by roughly 122 orders of magnitude.

This is not surprising because generalized entropy is expected to grow rather than remain conserved.
